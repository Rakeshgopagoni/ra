About Durga Mata Utsav Committee

Durga Mata Utsav Committee is a sacred and devotional organization formed with the blessings of Maa Durga to celebrate Navratri with faith and unity.

We organize Durga Puja celebrations, bhajans, cultural programs and spiritual gatherings.

Our mission is to preserve traditions and serve society through religious and social activities.

May Maa Durga bless all devotees with health, happiness and prosperity.
